# Harbour & Hearth — Synthetic Brand Brief

This is a fictional training scenario. It contains no real customer or business data.

## Business and Offer

Harbour & Hearth is a fictional neighbourhood bakery serving working adults near Singapore's central business district. The featured offer is the **Sunrise Breakfast Box** at **S$28**.

Each box contains:

- two butter croissants;
- two pandan buns;
- two bottled cold brews; and
- four paper napkins.

The box is prepared for next-morning collection. Customers place a pre-order by **6:00 pm on the previous day** using the placeholder link `https://example.com/sunrise-box`.

## Customer Action

The desired customer action is a completed pre-order for one or more Sunrise Breakfast Boxes.

## Operating Facts

- Collection is available from **7:30 am to 11:00 am** at the fictional Tanjong Pagar outlet.
- The bakery can fulfil a maximum of **40 boxes per day** during this training scenario.
- Delivery availability, allergen advice, weekend opening hours, refunds, and order-specific changes are not defined in this brief and must be treated as unknown.

## Brand Promise

Harbour & Hearth helps busy people organise a warm, simple team breakfast with a predictable pre-order and collection routine.

## Approved Proof

- The product contents, price, pre-order cut-off, collection window, and capacity stated above are approved synthetic facts.
- No customer testimonials, awards, health benefits, popularity claims, sales counts, or limited-time promotion are approved.

## Brand Voice

- Warm, direct sentences of 8 to 18 words.
- Start with a recognisable morning situation or a useful detail.
- Use Singapore English naturally without forced slang.
- Give one helpful detail before one invitation.
- Avoid exaggerated urgency, guilt, fear, and unsupported superlatives.

## Visual Direction

- Palette: deep teal, warm cream, sunrise orange, and charcoal.
- Natural morning light, uncluttered surfaces, and one clear focal point.
- Product representation must match the approved contents.
- Do not generate logos, customer faces, testimonials, awards, nutrition claims, or text baked into an image.

## Campaign Parameters

- Planning horizon: seven days.
- Practice budget: hypothetical S$40 per day; no live spend.
- Decision focus: completed pre-orders, cost per purchase, return on ad spend context, negative feedback, and fulfilment capacity.
- Public posting, customer messaging, targeting changes, and budget changes require an authorised human owner.

## Instagram Account Context

- The fictional brand uses an Instagram Professional account; the exact account ID, linked Meta assets, permissions, and production API configuration are not supplied.
- Feed posts, carousels, Stories, Reels, comments, direct messages, Insights, and Instagram ads may be planned in this scenario.
- Any interface path, API permission, publishing eligibility, messaging limit, or advertising setting must be verified by the authorised account owner before real use.
- No access token, password, customer identifier, or live account screenshot is part of this training scenario.
